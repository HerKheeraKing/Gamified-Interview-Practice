How a case session works

1

Pick a case

Open any question below — or let one find you at random.

2

Speak it out loud

Answer verbally first. Reading off a script doesn't build interview muscle — talking does.

3

Score it

Rate yourself honestly across five categories. This is practice, not a performance review.

4

Close the case

XP logs automatically. Watch your rank climb over the weeks, not the day.

The Scorecard

Each answer is scored 1–5 across five categories (25 pts possible), plus a +5 bonus for naming a real project with specifics.

  • Structure — Clear frame (Situation → Task → Action → Result), not a ramble.
  • Relevance — Actually answers the question asked.
  • Clarity & Delivery — Concise, confident, minimal filler.
  • Evidence — Specific details: tools, numbers, outcomes.
  • Impact / Close — Lands the point instead of trailing off.
